How these 3D shapes are generated

The Fibonacci sphere uses the golden angle (about 137.5°) to spread points evenly across the surface without clumping. Spherical harmonics reshape that sphere by modulating radius with angular sine/cosine terms to form lobed patterns.

Torus, shell, and Möbius strip shapes are built from parametric equations, where angles are converted into x/y/z coordinates and then scaled by the radius control so each shape remains centered and interactive with the same camera controls.
